## Overview

As DesignOnCue scales to serve SMBs and startups, strategic partnerships can:
- **Reduce costs** for hosting and services
- **Generate additional revenue** through commissions/referrals
- **Add value** to client projects without increasing overhead
- **Streamline delivery** through preferred vendor relationships

This document tracks partnership opportunities, terms, and evaluation criteria.

---

## Partnership Evaluation Criteria

When evaluating any agency partnership, consider:

### Financial
- [ ] **Cost to join:** Free or paid membership?
- [ ] **Discounts/benefits:** What do we get as partners?
- [ ] **Commission structure:** How much per referral/sale?
- [ ] **Revenue potential:** Realistic monthly/annual earnings?
- [ ] **Client pricing impact:** Can we pass savings to clients or increase margins?

### Service Quality
- [ ] **Platform reliability:** Uptime, performance, support quality
- [ ] **Client experience:** Easy for clients to use/manage?
- [ ] **White-label options:** Can we brand it as our service?
- [ ] **Support availability:** 24/7? Dedicated partner support?

### Integration & Workflow
- [ ] **Onboarding complexity:** How hard to set up clients?
- [ ] **Management tools:** Centralized dashboard for multiple clients?
- [ ] **API/automation:** Can we automate provisioning?
- [ ] **Billing flexibility:** Can we handle billing or pass-through to clients?

### Strategic Fit
- [ ] **Target market alignment:** Does this serve our SMB/startup clients?
- [ ] **Service complement:** Does it enhance our core offerings?
- [ ] **Exclusivity requirements:** Any conflicts with other partnerships?
- [ ] **Long-term viability:** Is this partner stable and growing?

## Hosting Partnerships

### 1. Bluehost Agency Partner Program

**Status:** Under Evaluation
**Program Type:** Free to Join
**Last Updated:** October 18, 2025

#### Program Overview

Bluehost offers an Agency Partner Program designed for freelancers and agencies building websites for clients.

**Official Program Page:** [Bluehost Agency Partners](https://www.bluehost.com/agency-partners)

#### Key Benefits

**For Partners (DesignOnCue):**
- **Free to join** - No upfront membership fees
- **Free Cloud Hosting year** - One free year of Cloud Hosting on their Cloud 1 plan (for testing/agency use)
- **High commission rates** - 75% of each qualified sale, capping at $500 per sale
- **Partner resources** - Access to agency-specific tools and support (details TBD)

**For Clients:**
- Potentially discounted hosting through agency partnership
- Reliable hosting backed by established provider
- Agency manages setup and configuration

#### Commission Structure

**Commission Terms:**
- **Starting rate:** 75% of qualified sale
- **Cap per sale:** $500 maximum
- **Qualified sale definition:** [To be confirmed with Bluehost sales team]
- **Payment terms:** [To be confirmed]
- **Tracking method:** [To be confirmed - likely referral links or partner dashboard]

**Revenue Potential:**
- If 10 clients sign up for hosting at ~$300/year each = up to $2,250 in commissions (75% of $3,000)
- Cap of $500/sale means large hosting deals max out quickly
- Best for volume (many clients) rather than high-value individual sales

#### Pricing Information

**Challenge:** Bluehost doesn't publish flat "agency hosting" pricing publicly.

**What We Know:**
- General shared hosting renewal pricing:
  - Starter: $15.99/month
  - Plus/Choice: Higher tiers available
- Cloud hosting (mentioned in free year benefit) - pricing TBD
- Agency-specific pricing requires **booking a call with sales team**

**Action Required:**
- Contact Bluehost sales to discuss:
  - Custom agency hosting plan pricing
  - Bulk client management options
  - White-label possibilities
  - Exact commission payout terms
  - Partner support availability

#### Pros & Cons

**Pros:**
- ✅ Free to join, no risk
- ✅ Strong commission rate (75%)
- ✅ Established brand (clients trust Bluehost)
- ✅ Free year of hosting for testing
- ✅ Suitable for WordPress sites (our common platform)

**Cons:**
- ❌ Commission caps at $500/sale (limits high-value deals)
- ❌ No transparent agency pricing (need sales call)
- ❌ Shared hosting may not be performant enough for some clients
- ❌ May lock us into recommending Bluehost even if not best fit
- ❌ Commission model requires client to purchase through our link (tracking complexity)

#### Use Cases for DesignOnCue

**Best for:**
- **SMB clients with basic WordPress sites** (5-7 page sites, low traffic)
- **Budget-conscious clients** who need affordable hosting
- **Clients who want managed WordPress hosting** without tech complexity

**Not ideal for:**
- **High-performance needs** (e-commerce with high traffic, complex apps)
- **Clients requiring custom server configs** (likely better with VPS or cloud hosting)
- **Next.js or headless CMS projects** (Vercel or Netlify better suited)

#### Integration into DesignOnCue Service Packages

**Potential Offering:**
- **Starter Studio package:** Include 1 year Bluehost hosting (using free Cloud 1 plan)
- **Growth/Super Studio packages:** Offer Bluehost as hosting option, collect commission
- **WordPress-specific clients:** Default recommendation
- **Custom app clients:** Use Vercel/Railway instead

**Pricing Strategy:**
1. **Pass-through model:** Client pays Bluehost directly, we earn commission
   - Transparent for client
   - Less revenue control for us

2. **Bundled model:** We charge flat hosting fee, we pay Bluehost, keep difference
   - Example: Charge client $300/year, pay Bluehost $180, keep $120 + earn $135 commission = $255 margin
   - More complex billing but higher margin

3. **Hybrid model:** Offer hosting setup as add-on service
   - Charge $500 setup fee (includes hosting setup + 1st year hosting)
   - Client renews directly with Bluehost after year 1
   - We earn commission on initial sale + setup fee

## Risks & Considerations

### Conflicts of Interest
**Risk:** Recommending partners for commission rather than best client fit
**Mitigation:**
- Always present multiple options to clients
- Document why we recommend specific partners
- Disclose commission relationships transparently (if required)

### Dependency
**Risk:** Over-reliance on one partner (e.g., all clients on Bluehost)
**Mitigation:**
- Maintain relationships with 2-3 hosting providers
- Platform-agnostic approach in sales conversations

### Commission Tracking
**Risk:** Lost or disputed commissions
**Mitigation:**
- Document all referral links and codes
- Screenshot confirmations and tracking dashboards
- Follow up on payments monthly

### Client Lock-in
**Risk:** Partners may lock clients into long-term contracts
**Mitigation:**
- Understand all contract terms before recommending
- Prefer month-to-month or annual (vs. multi-year) commitments
- Ensure clients own their data and can migrate easily
